Patrick X. Capaldo

AI Engineer @ Airbus · Chair AI TN, VC Aerospace TN, Author @ IET · Bristol, UK

Building assurable, effective, reliable, and resilient autonomous systems in safety-critical and highly-regulated industries, particularly within the defence and aerospace domains. With 6 years' experience in the defence and aerospace industries and as a current AI Engineer at Airbus, I have worked, and am working towards this by helping:

  • Build unsupervised anomaly detection systems for ground testing of the European Service Module's propulsion system for NASA's Artemis program (the powerhouse of the human-rated Orion spacecraft) [Airbus Defence & Space].
  • Automate and parallelise satellite software scripts for Airbus' OneSat geostationary communications satellite [Airbus Defence & Space].
  • Build an AI-augmented wing assembly assistant for the A350 (the world's most advanced long-haul aircraft) [Airbus Commercial].
  • Automate the ingestion, cleaning, and analysis of telemetry data from the SatVu / Surrey Satellite Technology HotSat thermal imaging satellite [SatVu].
  • Publish two research papers at two international conferences on the topic of explainable reinforcement learning (XRL) for aircraft manufacturing logistics (XAI World Conference as second author and EXPLAINS 2025 as first author and best paper nominee) [Airbus Commercial].
  • Develop a visualisation tool for the Royal Australian Air Force's Jindalee Operational Radar Network, the world leader in high-frequency, skywave over-the-horizon radar technology [Lockheed Martin].
  • Build distributed, autonomous, decision-support tools for the Royal Australian Air Force to optimise operator cognitive load [Defence Science and Technology Group].

In my spare time, I volunteer at the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) within the Aerospace and Artificial Intelligence Technical Networks to share and advance public knowledge in these fields and to engineer a better world.
